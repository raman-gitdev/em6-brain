# Carrier B1C06 (Japan domestic road)

## File layout
Workbook "B1C06 ... Rates", three sheets, currency JPY.
- "Consolidated Heiwajima" and "Consolidated Narashino.Tsukuba": consolidated (LTL) carriage. Same layout, different prices; the sheet name is the departure warehouse (origin).
- "Charter": dedicated trucks, all departure warehouses.

## Consolidated sheets
- Section 1 "Depart From ...": destination = district columns (Hokkaido, Tohoku A, Tohoku B, Kanto, Shinetsu, Tokai, Hokuriku, Kansai, Chugoku, Shikoku, Kyushu A, Kyushu B, Okinawa); rows = weight bands "Up to 1 kg" ... "Up to 100 kg", then "Over 100 kg add every 10 kg". Prices are per shipment for the band.
- Section 2: dangerous goods table (used when the shipment is DG under Japanese law), bands up to 30 kg. Note: revised if airline fares change.
- District map: Tohoku A = Aomori, Akita, Iwate; Tohoku B = Miyagi, Yamagata, Fukushima; Kyushu A = Fukuoka, Saga, Nagasaki, Oita; Kyushu B = Kumamoto, Miyazaki, Kagoshima.
- Notes: tax excluded; insurance excluded (5 JPY per 10,000 JPY declared value); volume weight 6000 cm3 = 1 kg, used when larger than actual weight; time service: next-day by 10:00 +200 JPY, by 9:00 +500 JPY.

## Charter sheet
- Rows = driving distance bands; columns = vehicle: light truck (its distance labels in column A), 2 ton, 4 ton, 11 ton (their distance labels in column E). Over 500 km: add a fixed amount per +50 km.
- Included: highway tolls, rear gate, air suspension. Excluded: ferry. Air-conditioned or cold-storage truck: +20%.
- Inter-warehouse fixed fares by truck size; incidental fees (work assistant, packing, unpacking ...); cancellation: day before 50%, same day 100%.
