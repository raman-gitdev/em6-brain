# Transport modes and services

## Road
- LTL / groupage / consolidated: shared truck, priced by weight band (and sometimes pallets or cbm), often by postcode or region.
- FTL / dedicated: whole truck, priced per truck by lane or distance band and vehicle size.
- Charter: a vehicle booked for one job, priced by distance and vehicle size; tolls may be included or not.
- Parcel: small packages, zone x weight.

## Air
- General air freight: priced per kg by weight break (+45, +100, +300, +500, +1000), with minimums; fuel and security surcharges per kg.
- Express / courier: door-to-door, zone x weight (0.5 kg steps for light items), volume divisor often 5000.
- Lane carriers price airport-to-airport or country-to-country; zone carriers use a zone chart.
- Service scope: DTD, DTA, ATA, ATD (what legs are included).

## Sea
- FCL: price per container (20', 40', 40'HC, reefer).
- LCL: price per W/M (weight or measure, 1 cbm = 1000 kg), with minimums.
- Common extras: BAF (fuel), THC (terminal handling), documentation, demurrage/detention.

## Transit and service levels
Transit time is the promised duration. Service levels (economy, standard, priority, next-flight-out ...) are carrier-specific; never assume two carriers mean the same thing by the same name.
