# Surcharges and accessorials

## Fuel surcharge (FSC)
Changes with fuel prices. Three common methods:
- Published %: the carrier publishes a weekly or monthly % applied to freight (sometimes with a contract discount on that %).
- Index table: an official fuel price (diesel for road, jet fuel for air) for a period is looked up in a table of price bands that gives a % or an amount per kg.
- Formula: e.g. (average diesel price - base price) / base price x a factor.
Watch for: which week/month applies (often the ship date, sometimes with a lag), what base the % applies to (freight only, or freight + some surcharges), and whether fuel is already included.

## Dangerous goods (DG / hazmat)
DG shipments may use a separate price table, a % surcharge with a maximum, or a fixed fee. Not all carriers accept all DG classes.

## Other surcharges
- Peak season, war risk, security (air, per kg), handling, terminal charges.
- Remote / out-of-delivery area (ODA), island delivery, ferry.
- Temperature-controlled / refrigerated (reefer) vehicles, often + a %.
- Oversize / out of gauge (OOG), heavy pieces, non-stackable.

## Accessorials (extra services)
Pickup, delivery appointment, tail-lift, waiting/detention time, residential delivery, packing/unpacking, extra workers, day-before loading, customs clearance, inside delivery, re-delivery, cancellation fee.

## Taxes and insurance
Consumption tax / VAT / GST is usually excluded from tariffs. Cargo insurance is usually extra, often a rate per declared value.
